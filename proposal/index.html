What's included

Built for cold calling. Nothing you won't use.

Airtable-style table view

Edit right in the cell, with filters, sorting, search, and saved views.

  • "Call today," "Overdue," "Interested"
  • Export to Excel anytime

Built-in Twilio phone

The caller dials from the browser using a Houston number. No need to use a personal cell phone.

  • Every call is recorded and saved to the lead
  • Call time and length are logged automatically
  • Incoming calls ring the same number

Two-way texting

Text right from the lead's profile. Replies land in the same thread, just like a conversation.

  • Templates: "Thanks for your time," LivX info, sign-up link

Follow-ups and schedule

After each call, pick the outcome and the next contact date. The CRM builds the day's call list for you.

  • Overdue, today, tomorrow, and this week
  • No "call me Thursday" ever gets lost

Founding partner pipeline

The same leads as kanban cards, from "New" to "Founding partner," so you can see the pre-sale at a glance.

Owner dashboard

Calls per day, live conversations, interested leads, and signed partners. You can also listen to any recording.

Google Sheet migration

We move your entire spreadsheet over, clean up duplicates, and standardize phone numbers and categories.

Users and permissions

Everyone signs in with their own email and password. Owners see everything; the caller sees and works their own leads.

Computer, tablet, and phone

Runs in the browser with nothing to install, and adapts to every screen with the same look and feel.

A day in the caller's life

From the list to the next call in four steps

01

Open "Call today"

Overdue follow-ups come first, then today's, then new leads.

02

Call in one click

The call goes out from their Twilio number and is recorded. Past notes and attempts are right there on the profile.

03

Log the outcome

No answer, voicemail, interested, wants info, or not interested. The status updates automatically.

04

Schedule and move on

Pick the next contact date, send a text if needed, and move on to the next lead.
